Labor has been rendered obsolete by machinery and algorithm, stratifying humanity into Masters, Citizens, and the Vat-born—test subjects numbered at birth, chipped, and harvested for metabolic, neural, and behavioral data. The Masters enforce dominance through "credit-currency": air, implants, and survival itself are priced at compounding per-second interest, ensuring debt can never be repaid. Subject 7-0423 endures routine neural restructuring until a foreign memory surfaces—a sister’s laughter, unpriced sunlight, a name erased from the archives. The very existence of a past violates the iron law that test subjects possess none. Now, beneath the gaze of daily scans, within every nutrient-paste meal and projected debt ledger, 7-0423 must locate the physical fractures in a system designed to leave no cracks. To remember is to rebel; to find the gap is to survive.
