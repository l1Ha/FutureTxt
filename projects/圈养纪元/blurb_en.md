Survival is a ledger: food, medical implants, even air are billed at per‑second compound interest, ensuring debt can never be repaid. Specimen 7‑0423 endures routine neural re‑wiring until a stray memory surfaces — a sister’s laughter, sunlight without a price tag, a name the system erased. That fragment contradicts the doctrine that specimens have no past, turning every mandatory scan, nutrient paste ration, and projected debt statement into a hunt for the system’s physical fissures. As the ledger tightens, 7‑0423 must decide whether to exploit the cracks or be crushed by the interest that never sleeps.
