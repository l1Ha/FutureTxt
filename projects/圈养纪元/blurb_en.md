In a world where labor has been entirely outsourced to machines and algorithms, humanity has fractured into three castes: the creditor‑elite who price every breath, the nominally free who scrape by on credit, and the “subjects” — numbered, chipped, and bred for endless pharmacological, neural, and behavioral trials. Subject 7‑0423 endures routine neural re‑wiring until a stray memory surfaces: a sister’s laughter, sunlight that carries no price tag, a name erased from every ledger. That fragment violates the iron law that subjects have no past, and it turns every mandatory scan, every nutrient paste ration, every compounding debt notice into a covert hunt for the system’s physical seams. As the algorithmic ledger tightens its grip, 7‑0423 must decide whether to exploit the glitch and reclaim a self the regime denies, or be consumed by a debt that compounds by the second.
