# Chapter 5: Broadcast Storm: The Unpriced Name

The faint sweet‑oil scent of the Respiratory Lubrication System is compressed into a thin mist in the recirculating air of the orbital station **Apollo‑9**’s core compute cabin. It slips through the nasal passages into the lungs, carrying an industrial cloy. The cold metal cuff at the ankle (≈ 12 °C) presses against the skin like a fine chain of ice; each heartbeat conducts the chill to the fingertips, reminding **Subject 7‑0423** that he is still a number, still quantified. The Retinal Bill Crimson Digit Stream still scrolls 0.8 m before his eyes—principal 1.2 × 10⁵ CR compounding per millisecond at 0.00012 %/s (W2), pushing the figures deeper into the red. The laboratory’s white noise and the low drone of centrifuges seep in from afar, a gray vocal cord vibrating against the eardrums. The bitter aftertaste of compulsory medication lingers on the tongue; the metallic chew resistance of the breakfast Nutrient Paste Synthesizer (1.8 N·mm) still produces a faint “crunch” between the teeth. The uniform friction of the Automated Cleaning/Maintenance Robot Swarm rolling down the corridor (≈ 38 dB) reverberates through the whole level, as if the entire **Debt Tower** were holding its breath for the imminent circuit‑breaker. Condensation beads slide down alloy panels, releasing the lightest “drip… drip,” intertwining with the deep hum of the distant fusion reactors into a stifled rhythm.  

**Subject 7‑0423** crouches before the server array. Three parallel scars on his palm ooze dark red; his fingernails have gouged a shallow groove in the metal wall. He exploits the pain peak to force heart rate, cortisol, and neural potential into the “compliance” band; any deviation would be flagged as a violation, instantly triggering a **Debt Reset** and an escalation of experiment intensity (W7). The **Global Physiological Monitoring Network (W6)** uploads every breath, every micro‑tremor in real time; the data stream is adjudicated millisecond‑by‑millisecond in the **Central Clearing Core**’s **Compute Palace**. Sweat slides down his cheekbones, meeting blood at the chin, forming a dark‑red trace that falls into the corridor dust, stirring an almost imperceptible vortex. He feels the violent shudder of the heat‑sink at the nape; a hot current climbs the spine like an invisible serpent threading the spinal cord.  

**Maintainer “Old Chen”** flickers behind a concealed panel, the worn data port behind his left ear pulsing faintly. He taps the panel in a low voice: “The window’s still open—don’t let the monitors catch the fluctuation.” His words carry the tremor of a rusted iron plate struck by a sledgehammer. In his mind he repeats: *“If this 0.7 s isn’t cut, my daughter’s loan can be pushed another day.”* **Subject 7‑0423** does not turn; he merely smears the blood on his fingertips across the cold metal, using that thin layer of temperature to keep his tactile sense sharp. The heat‑sink at his nape shudders again; heat climbs the spine, sweat and blood merge on the cheekbones, a dark‑red line tracing the jaw and dropping into the dust, spawning another microscopic vortex.  

In the same millisecond, the iris‑projection ring of **Supervisor 9‑A “Vera”** flares a ghostly blue; the **Data Integrity Check** on her audit terminal flashes “Check Failed.” Her fingertip hovers over the report key; heart rate spikes from 78 bpm to 112 bpm, cortisol rises 27 % in 0.3 s. The contract clauses for her daughter’s rare‑disease drug discount scroll through her thoughts; interest and rule wrestle in that fleeting hesitation. She finally chooses to delay the report, granting the broadcast a sliver of chance. Her breathing quickens; fingertips whiten on the key, as though pressing the weight of the world into a single click.  

The face‑projection band of **Auditor “Zero”** shows the crimson legend **“Purge Complete 99%”** shudder; the compute core suffers a 0.4 s fluctuation—coolant pressure drops 12 %, three fusion reactors’ output jitter 0.3 % in the same frame. For the first time the **Slaveholder** class loses absolute control of the data flow. Zero’s protocol attempts a highest‑privilege overwrite of the broadcast (W2 **Debt Reset** manual override), but the broadcast has already fragmented across the neural interface caches of thirty thousand **Test Subjects**; overwriting requires individual purge, physically impossible within 0.7 s. Its synthetic voice echoes in the corridor, cold as a blade on metal: “Unauthorized write flagged. Preparing W2 override protocol. Requires per‑subject purge. Estimated 14 h.”  

**Subject 7‑0419 “Xiao Liu”**’s cervical pulse ring overloads; a high‑frequency vibration pierces the monitoring blind spot, plucking the neural rhythm of every **Test Subject** in the bay like an invisible string. All retinal debt streams simultaneously display the three characters **“Lin Wei”**, accompanied by an unpriced sensory fragment of sunlight: temperature 23 °C, air scented with grass and dust, the slight give of soft earth underfoot—synchronised within 0.7 s. The system flags a “Data Integrity Anomaly” and triggers a network‑wide **Data Circuit Breaker Protocol (W9)**: write locks, debt ledger freeze, forced experiment pause for 6 h.  

The breaker countdown rolls on every retina in pale blue digits: **5:59:59…** The compound‑interest pointer is frozen in amber time; **Free Citizen** dividends halt; the **Memory/Emotion Archive & Trading Platform (T4)** cannot settle. The cleaning robot 07‑T continues its even friction, yet beneath it a barely perceptible low‑frequency resonance appears— the first synchronized neural cadence of thousands of **Test Subjects**. The air carries a trace of ozone mixed with the lubricant’s cloy, as if the whole station were holding its breath for the next beat.  

**Subject 7‑0423** feels the scarred palm stop bleeding; a strange warmth spreads from his fingertips, like sunlight through glass on skin. He no longer gouges his palm with nails; instead he brushes the cold metal cuff at his ankle, confirming that the reality he senses comes from the shared memory, not self‑harm. The retinal ledger resumes scrolling; each line now ends with a faint “★” metadata tag— a bit the system never defined. That star flickers in the crimson sea of numbers, an unauthorized punctuation stubbornly occupying the end of every row.  

At the instant the breaker reaches zero, a fresh whiff of ozone surges through the corridor, mingling with the low drone of robot 07‑T’s motors, as though some massive heart were restarting in a steel chest. The crimson stream rolls again; the compound‑interest pointer clicks forward, yet every line still bears that undefined “★”, faint but obstinate.  

**Supervisor 9‑A “Vera”**’s voice comes from behind the audit terminal, hoarse, fingertip still poised over the report key, the ghost‑blue halo in her eyes contracting: “Data Integrity Check passed… but this marker isn’t in the rules.” Her gaze sweeps the screen of stars, brow furrowing as she weighs whether to launch an emergency protocol.  

On **Auditor “Zero”**’s projection band the crimson “Purge Complete 100%” has become “Purge Complete 100%” yet stalls on the final frame; the red characters’ edges sprout a halo of dark‑gray noise. Its synthetic voice repeats, cold as a blade on metal: “Unauthorized write flagged. Preparing W2 override protocol. Requires per‑subject purge. Estimated 14 h.”

On the retinal ledger the solitary “★” begins to spread left and right at visible speed, a ribbon of micro‑light creeping through the crimson digits— heralding that the next circuit‑breaker will be detonated pre‑emptively at the data layer. The monitoring light at the corridor’s far end flickers once; robot 07‑T’s tires roll over dust, raising a string of tiny vortices, as if applauding the unfinished game.  

Far off the fusion reactors continue their low moan; coolant flowing through pipes sounds like a slumbering behemoth breathing. **Subject 7‑0423** lifts his head, gazing through the transparent bulkhead at the starfield; starlight pierces the thin mist of the viewport, spilling faint patches onto the metal deck. He senses the star on his palm warming, as if each heartbeat draws him nearer to that unpriced name. At the corridor’s end **Maintainer “Old Chen”** has vanished behind the concealed panel, leaving only an unfinished series of taps echoing in the metal ducts—a promise reverberating in steel. **Supervisor 9‑A “Vera”** finally lifts her fingertip; the screen flashes green “Check Passed,” yet a lone, unrecorded “★” blinks in the corner. **Auditor “Zero”**’s projection freezes on the last frame; the noise at the red text’s edges expands, threatening to devour the entire purge announcement.  

In this forest of steel woven from data and debt, the only thing unquantified is that sliver of sunlight’s warmth, the scent of grass, the yielding of earth—and the shared, undefined star flickering on every **Test Subject**’s retinal ledger, at the tail of every debt line, stubbornly glowing.
