# Chapter 5: Broadcast Storm: The Unpriced Name

7‑0423 crouched before the server array, three parallel scars on his palm seeping dark red; his fingernails had gouged a shallow trench in the metal bulkhead. The heat‑sink fins at the nape of his neck trembled violently, a hot current climbing his spine, sweat and blood meeting at his cheekbones and forming a single dark‑red trace that fell into the corridor dust, stirring an almost imperceptible vortex. The air carried the sweet‑metallic scent of coolant and the charred, caramelized odor of old circuit boards; each breath felt like swallowing a mouthful of rusted iron filings.  

He used the pain peak to force his heart rate, cortisol, and neural potentials into the “compliance” band; any deviation would be flagged as a violation, instantly triggering a Debt Reset and an escalation of experiment intensity (W7). The Global Physiological Monitoring Network (W6) uploaded every breath, every micro‑tremor in real time; the data stream was adjudicated millisecond by millisecond in the Central Clearing Core (CCC), a crimson judgment cursor flashing across the screen as if pricing each heartbeat. Fine white mist sprayed from the cooling vents at the screen’s edge, coating his pale face like a thin frost.  

Lao Chen’s figure flickered behind a concealed panel, the worn data port behind his left ear pulsing faintly, the metal housing’s edges etched with fine rust lines from years of service. He whispered into the panel, “The window’s still open—don’t let the monitor catch a fluctuation.” His voice carried the tremor of a rusted iron plate struck by a heavy hammer, each word echoing like Morse code through metal conduits. 7‑0423 did not turn; he merely smeared the blood from his fingertips onto the cold bulkhead, using that thin layer of warmth to keep his palm’s tactile sense alert; his fingertip brushed a protruding bolt head, the cold hardness confirming he still stood on reality’s edge. The fingertip slid over the bolt, leaving a faint metallic rasp, as if whispering to an unseen contract.  

In the same millisecond, Regulator Vira’s right‑eye iris projection ring flared a ghostly blue; the audit terminal flashed “Data Integrity Check – Failed.” Her fingertip hovered over the report key, breathing rapid, the nail leaving a shallow imprint on the keycap’s edge; she finally chose to delay the report, buying the broadcast a sliver of life. The red warning on the screen pulsed like a heartbeat, each flash contracting her pupils. Her other hand unconsciously brushed the condensation beads on the terminal’s rim, the fingertip feeling a bite of cold climb up her wrist.  

Auditor “Zero”’s facial projection band displayed the blood‑red legend “Cleansing Complete 100%, Circuit Breaker Initiated” which suddenly jittered; the compute core exhibited a 0.4 s fluctuation—coolant pressure dropped 12 %, three fusion reactors’ output power jittered 0.3 % in the same frame. Zero’s synthetic voice reverberated through the corridor, cold and sharp as a blade scraping metal: “Unauthorized write marked; preparing to initiate W2 Overwrite Protocol. Gradual freeze per entity during breaker window.” The voice held no temperature, yet it set the metal walls vibrating faintly, as if the entire station trembled at the proclamation. The wall coating wrinkled under the sound wave, revealing the dark‑gray alloy grain beneath.  

Xiao Liu’s neck pulse ring overloaded, a high‑frequency vibration piercing the monitoring blind zone, like an invisible string plucking the neural rhythm of every test subject in the bay. All test subjects’ retinal billboards simultaneously displayed the three characters “Lin Wei” in a blood‑red digital stream, accompanied by an unpriced sensory fragment of sunlight: temperature 23 °C, the mixed scent of grass and dust, the slight give of soft earth underfoot—synchronized only within the already‑missed 0.7 s firmware window. The system ruled it a “Data Integrity Anomaly,” triggering a network‑wide “Data Circuit Breaker” (W9): write locks engaged, debt ledgers frozen, experiments forcibly paused for 6 h.  

The breaker countdown rolled in pale blue digits on every retina: **5:59:59…** The compound‑interest pointer of debt was suspended in amber time; Freeperson dividends halted; the Memory‑Emotion Archive & Trading Platform could not settle. Corridor Cleaning Robot 07‑T’s whir remained steady, yet beneath it a barely perceptible low‑frequency resonance emerged—a collective neural cadence of thousands of test subjects syncing for the first time. The air carried a trace of ozone mixed with the sweet‑metallic coolant, numbing the nasal passages.  

7‑0423 felt the bleeding at his palm scars cease; a strange warmth rose at his fingertips, like sunlight through glass on skin. He no longer raked his nails across his palm; instead he pressed his palm flat against the bulkhead, confirming that the sense of reality now came from a shared memory, not self‑harm. The retinal billboard scrolled anew; each line of numbers now bore a faint “★” at its end—a metadata bit the system had never defined. The star flickered in the sea of blood‑red digits, an unauthorized punctuation stubbornly occupying every line’s terminus; its halo cast a microscopic rainbow fringe on the retina, as if each heartbeat added a thin glaze.  

Vira’s voice came from behind the audit terminal, hoarse, fingertip still poised over the report key, the ghostly blue halo in her eyes receding: “Data Integrity Check passed, but… this marker isn’t in the rules.” Her gaze swept the star‑filled screen, brow furrowing as she weighed whether to trigger an emergency protocol; her fingertip brushed the keycap lightly, producing the faintest plastic rasp, like caressing a fragile piece of glass.  

On Zero’s projection band, the blood‑red “Cleansing Complete 100%” froze on the final frame, a ring of dark‑gray noise creeping around the crimson characters. Its synthetic voice sounded again: “Unauthorized write marked; preparing to initiate W2 Overwrite Protocol. Gradual freeze per entity during breaker window.” The tone remained flat, yet a faint metallic echo rose at the corridor’s far end, as if the entire passage shuddered at the decree. The projection band’s edge began to peel pixel by pixel, exposing the underlying gray‑black substrate.  

7‑0423 did not turn; he merely replayed the temperature, scent, and tactile feel of that sunlight memory in his mind, fingertips tracing the rim of the metal ring, sensing a weak current flow from fingertip to palm. He spoke low toward the corridor’s depths: “As long as ★ remains, the debt will forever lack one decimal place.” The words were quiet, yet they set the metal walls resonating faintly, each syllable etching a mark for the unpriced name. The vibration traveled through the conduits to the distant coolant pump, which emitted a single deep, muffled thump.  

Xiao Liu’s pulse ring accelerated sharply, emitting a piercing shriek before settling into calm. On the retinal billboard, the solitary “★” began spreading left and right at visible speed, like rows of micro‑light creeping through the blood‑red numbers, heralding that the next breaker would be pre‑emptively detonated at the data layer. The monitoring lights at the corridor’s end flickered once; Cleaning Robot 07‑T’s tires rolled over dust, kicking up a tiny vortex of particles, as if applauding the unfinished contest. Dust danced in the light beams, refracting shattered rainbows.  

The distant fusion reactors continued their low hum; coolant flowing through pipes sounded like a slumbering leviathan breathing. 7‑0423 lifted his head, gazing through the transparent bulkhead at the starfield; starlight filtered through the viewport mist, spilling weak speckles onto the metal deck. He felt the star on his palm warming, as if each heartbeat drew him nearer to that unpriced name. At the corridor’s far end, Lao Chen’s silhouette had vanished behind the hidden panel, leaving only an unfinished series of taps echoing like a promise in the metal ducts. Vira’s fingertip finally left the report key; the screen’s “Check Passed” green light illuminated, yet a solitary, unrecorded “★” blinked in the corner. Zero’s projection froze on the last frame, the noise fringe around the red text expanding gradually, as if poised to consume the entire “Cleansing Complete” declaration.  

In this steel forest woven from data and debt, the only thing left unquantified is that sliver of sunlight’s warmth, the scent of grass, the faint give of earth—and the undefined star that now flashes in common on every test subject’s retina, at the tail of every debt ledger.
