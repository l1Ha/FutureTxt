“The log says you personally ordered the synthesis,” E‑11’s voice was low, gravel scraping in her throat. “I want the fear‑synthesis command revoked and the quota restored.”

E‑11 clutched the certificate; the paper’s coolness seeped into her fingertips, crawling up her nerves. The scar on her left forearm flared with a sudden current, as if reminding her: every compromise paves the road for the next gamble. She looked up—Han Ce’s left eye still pulsed at thirteen hertz, the blue‑green points tracing slender arcs in the white light, a silent countdown, an ancient ritual beacon.  

The distant fans continued their low roar; ozone grew thicker, mingling with the burnt‑oil scent that circled her nasal passages. E‑11 slipped the certificate into an inner pocket, fingertips brushing the cold alloy clasp; metal hardness met body heat, forming a faint current loop, a fine numbness spreading across her fingertips.

Metal grating underfoot emitted a soft “shush‑shush” as she walked; ozone and burnt oil intensified, each breath feeling like inhaling cold iron filings, throat tightening.
