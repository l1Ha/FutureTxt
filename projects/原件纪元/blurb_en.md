In 2126 the world runs on programmable quotas, and the only thing that cannot be forged is a verified provenance chain. Human experience has become the most valuable commodity: “originals” — people logged from birth and never edited — are confined to extraction pods where they must manufacture raw emotions such as rage, grief, and terror on demand, feeding the virtual realms and the intelligent agents that now govern labor. Veteran original E‑11 has mastered the art of summoning precise feelings, her body scarred by years of harvest. When a new order tags a fragment of fear as hers — a memory she does not possess — she must deliver the quota or face a frozen quota that would erase her livelihood. Caught between the provenance layer’s relentless audit, the predictive governance of the platform city‑states, and the fractured recesses of her own mind, E‑11 races to uncover the source of a fear the system insists is authentically hers.
