In 2126 the planet has been reduced to server farms and logistics corridors, and money has become programmable quota—whoever writes the rules of the ledger rules society. To stop lossless forgery a “Provenance Layer” binds every identity, memory and experience to an immutable source chain, turning unedited human life into the most valuable commodity. “Originals” like E‑11 are born into the system, their raw emotions—rage, grief, terror—harvested in sealed pods and sold as seed data for virtual worlds and autonomous agents, while the donors receive almost nothing. E‑11 has mastered the art of summoning precise feelings on command, her body scarred by years of extraction. A new contract, however, demands a fragment of fear she cannot recall. To meet the quota and avoid a quota freeze, she must navigate the Provenance Layer’s audits, the predictive governance of the platform city‑states, and the fractured corridors of her own memory to discover the true origin of the fear the system insists is hers.
